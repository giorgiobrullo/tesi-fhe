PBS-only and CM-KS+PBS are independent sample sets; their median inversion is measurement
noise and must not be interpreted as a negative key-switch cost. p4/w4 therefore provides
no convincing drop-in gain once one packing step is included.

GO / NO-GO FOR THE NEXT POC
  GO after componentwise POC only if:
    - every tested 2-bit state decrypts correctly across fresh keys;
    - the measured path performs no per-slot extraction;
    - bridge + CM stage is at least 1.5x faster than ordinary;
    - key payload and peak RSS are both reported.

  GO for full circuit work only if a four-slot OR/prefix reduction:
    - remains common-mask between levels;
    - is exact on exhaustive Boolean inputs;
    - retains an end-to-end latency advantage including all transformations.

  NO-GO if cross-slot work requires extraction/repacking at every level or removes the
  ordinary-vs-common-mask margin. The present measurements alone do not pass this gate.
